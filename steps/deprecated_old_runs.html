The first NYGC-based run gave five new PBS peaks, but they were artefacts of my extraction: the sites lie inside repeats or next to overlapping indels in the NYGC call set (for example CA-repeat deletions at 2:30673774, a 12-bp insertion at 22:48293814), so single-base records were not comparable with the array genotypes; the Uzbek array and 1000G phase 3 agree with each other at these sites. After excluding every site with an overlapping indel, multiallelic or structural-variant record within 1 bp (8,573 of 82,744 sites), PBS finds no Tier 1, 2 or 3 candidate (maximum PBS 0.07). Conclusion: no population-specific selection signal is supported. Check used: all-site scan for Uzbek frequencies outside the range of the four reference populations (6 of 81,986 sites, five of them these peaks).
ADMIXTURE and PCA, cohort of 1,047 (3,759 samples with reference; 60,019 LD-pruned SNPs; K=2–8, 3 seeds)currentReference populations separate cleanly at K=4. CV error has no clear minimum (elbow at K=4–5); at K=7 about half of the Uzbek ancestry forms a component absent from the references. Self-reported ethnicity and birthplace are both significantly associated with the ancestry components.
ADMIXTURE and PCA, expanded cohort of 1,256 (3,968 samples with reference; 64,959 LD-pruned SNPs; K=2–8, 2 seeds)currentSame picture as the 1,047 cohort: Uzbek ancestry at K=4 about 50% EUR-like, 30% EAS-like, 19% SAS-like; ethnicity and birthplace significantly associated with the components. Figures: see step11.
