Step 15: Runs of Homozygosity & IBD

Autozygosity mapping and identity-by-descent analysis — consanguinity, founder effects, and cryptic relatedness in the Uzbek cohort

🧬 Step 15 of 15 • 1,256 samples (expanded cohort) • 5,358,770 SNPs 📅 August 2026 ← View original 1,047-sample cohort results (archived, not directly comparable on this page)

1. Overview

Cohort expansion (August 2026): This page reflects the expanded cohort (1,256 samples). A real bug was found and fixed this round: the initial IBD run silently returned 0 pairs in every category because --extract was pointed at a pruned-marker file using chr:pos:ref:alt IDs against a dataset using rsIDs — a script-level 2>&1 | tail -20 pipe also masked the failure from set -e. Fixed by pointing at the correct matching-ID dataset and adding pipefail. See integration notes for details. ROH itself (a separate invocation, unaffected by the bug) was correct on the first run.

Runs of Homozygosity (ROH) are contiguous stretches of homozygous genotypes that arise when an individual inherits two copies of the same ancestral haplotype — a hallmark of parental relatedness (consanguinity) or population-level founder effects. Identity-by-Descent (IBD) analysis detects pairs of individuals sharing long haplotype segments, revealing cryptic relatedness not apparent from pedigree records.

Both analyses are critical for understanding the Uzbek population's demographic history and for designing association studies (e.g., pregnancy loss GWAS) where cryptic relatedness inflates test statistics if unaccounted for.

2. ROH Summary Statistics

PLINK 1.9 --homozyg was run on the Uzbek post-QC dataset (1,256 × 5,358,770 SNPs, expanded cohort) with parameters tuned for detecting long ROH segments reflecting recent consanguinity:

4. ROH by Chromosome

ROH frequency varies by chromosome, reflecting both chromosome length and regional recombination rate differences. Longer chromosomes accumulate more ROH simply because they contain more physical sequence, but recombination coldspots (e.g., pericentromeric regions) can elevate local ROH density beyond length expectations.

Notable: Chromosome 1 has the highest ROH count (5,495, expanded cohort), consistent with its large physical size (249 Mb). Chromosome 21 has the fewest ROH (208), reflecting its small size (46.7 Mb). The original cohort's top chromosome was chr2 (3,590) — this shift is a genuine observation from the larger sample, not yet further investigated.

Cross-method admixture-aware validation (May 2026): The merged ALSU+GWAS2026 dataset (1,193 samples, 104,411 LD-pruned SNPs) was analysed with four independent relatedness methods. Key results:
  • 6,228 pairs with PI_HAT ≥ 0.185; 4,494 were PI_HAT-only discordant (absent from KING at kinship ≥ 0.0884 — discordance rate 72%).
  • PI_HAT-only discordant pairs showed higher Step 11 K=5 Q-vector distances (mean 0.160 vs. 0.130 for concordant pairs), consistent with ancestry-structure inflation of PI_HAT.
  • PC-Relate supported only 5 of 4,494 PI_HAT-only pairs (kinship ≥ 0.0884).
  • RelateAdmix supported only 1 of 4,494 PI_HAT-only pairs.
  • Recommendation: use KING as the primary operational screen; treat PI_HAT alone as a diagnostic, not as grounds for sample removal in this admixed dataset.

7. ADMIXTURE × ROH Cross-Reference

Updated with expanded-cohort data (2026-08-15): Step 11's Uzbek-only ADMIXTURE rerun (single seed, K=2–8) is now complete — see Step 11 for the full methodology and CV-error stability results. This section now reflects the expanded cohort's own K=2 Q-values, not the original cohort's.

Using ADMIXTURE K=2 ancestry proportions (Q2: European-like component, mean 0.615; Q1: East Asian-like component, mean 0.385; expanded cohort, 1,256 samples), we can examine whether autozygosity varies by ancestry proportion. In admixed populations, individuals with more homogeneous ancestry (one dominant component) may show elevated homozygosity due to assortative mating within subgroups.

Expected pattern: In a single-pulse admixture, FROH should be somewhat elevated at both extremes of the ancestry distribution (Q1 < 0.3 or Q1 > 0.8) where individuals are genetically more homogeneous, and lower in the middle where recombination between ancestral haplotypes breaks up long homozygous blocks. Deviations suggest ongoing substructure or community-level endogamy.

8. Clinical Relevance for Pregnancy Loss

Clinical context: Elevated autozygosity (FROH) has been consistently associated with adverse reproductive outcomes including recurrent pregnancy loss (RPL), stillbirth, and congenital anomalies. The mechanisms include:
  • Increased homozygosity for recessive lethal or sub-lethal alleles
  • Reduced heterozygosity at HLA genes → impaired maternal–fetal immune tolerance
  • Increased burden of damaging homozygous variants in developmentally critical genes

Key takeaways for the Uzbek cohort

Population-level

  • Median FROH = 0.0147 — comparable to outbred European and South Asian populations, nearly identical to the original cohort (0.0148)
  • ~2.8% of individuals (35) have FROH > 0.0625, suggesting 1st-cousin-level parental consanguinity
  • Minimal cryptic relatedness (11 pairs at 2nd degree or closer in 1,256 samples)
  • Consistent with historical preference for endogamous marriages in Uzbek communities

GWAS design implications

  • Covariates required: FROH should be included as a covariate in pregnancy loss GWAS to control for genome-wide recessive burden
  • Kinship filter: Remove one individual from each pair with PI_HAT > 0.125, or use GRM-based mixed models
  • ROH-enriched genes: Loci consistently within ROH across affected women may harbor recessive pregnancy loss genes
  • Stratification: Consider analyzing high-FROH and low-FROH groups separately

9. Methods

Software: PLINK v1.9.0-b.7.7 (64-bit, 515 GB RAM workstation)
Input (ROH): UZB_imputed_HQ_qc BED/BIM/FAM (1,256 × 5,358,770 SNPs, post-QC, expanded cohort)
Input (IBD): UZB_imputed_HQ_unique + UZB_pruned.prune.in (1,256 × 87,573 SNPs, LD-pruned)
FROH formula: Σ(ROH length in kb) / 2,881,033 kb
IBD method: PLINK method-of-moments estimator (--genome flag)
Degree thresholds: Duplicates > 0.98, 1st > 0.354, 2nd > 0.177, 3rd > 0.0884
